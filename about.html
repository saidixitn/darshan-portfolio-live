About me

I'm Darshan, the person behind the cuts.

I'm a video editor from Vizag. I started with a free app and a lot of curiosity, and I never stopped, because I love the moment when a messy pile of clips suddenly starts to feel like something.

I work best with creators who care about their content as much as I do. I'll ask questions, give honest opinions, and treat your video like it's mine.

Outside editing, I'm usually watching films for the transitions or out near the beach with a camera.

How we'll work together

1. Tell me your idea. Send me the footage and a quick note on what you want people to feel.

2. I make the first cut. You get a draft that already feels close.

3. We refine it together. Your feedback shapes it until it's exactly right.

4. You publish with confidence. Delivered on time, in the right format.

Why creators work with me

✓ Clear communication. You always know where your project stands.

✓ On-time delivery. Most videos ready in 2–3 days.

✓ Revisions included. We don't stop until you love it.

✓ I care about the story, not just the software.

What I do for you:
Video Editing

Smooth cuts, strong pacing, clean sound. Your videos hold attention from the first second. Simple animations and text that make your message easier to follow, never harder. Visuals that make people curious enough to click. Reels and Shorts built to fit each platform, so your content stays consistent without eating your time.
