Who am I. Based in India, focused on Video Direction, Visual Editing, and Narrative Storytelling. I craft videos, narrative visual edits, and high-impact digital content with care, rhythm, and distinct personality. What drives me. Signature rhythm, precise typography, and meticulous pacing. I embed myself in the core feeling of the idea, translating emotion into visual form that cannot be ignored.
