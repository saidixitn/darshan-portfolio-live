About me

Hey, I'm Darshan.

I'm based in Vizag and work across video editing, visual design and social media. I enjoy making things, playing with ideas and turning a blank canvas into something of my own.

I'm always trying something new, experimenting with styles and finding little ways to make my work better. There's always something interesting to create, and that's what keeps me going.

How I work

I like keeping things simple, but never boring.

I experiment, try different ideas and pay attention to the little details. I don't like doing everything the same way - every project deserves its own style.

How I work:
Simple.

Keeping things simple, but never boring. Paying attention to the little things — the right cut, a font that fits, and colours that work together. Every project has its own mood and idea. Figuring out what works best without repeating styles. Experiment, make changes, and keep going until it feels right.
